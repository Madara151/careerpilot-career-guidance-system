How This Works

Your skills and each career's required skills are converted into weighted vectors using TF-IDF, then compared with cosine similarity — the same underlying technique used in many recommendation systems. Skills that are rare across careers count for more than skills nearly every career shares, so the match reflects what actually distinguishes each career, not just overlap count.
