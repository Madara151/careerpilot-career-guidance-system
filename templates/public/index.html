WHY CAREERPILOT EXISTS

Built at a desk exactly like this one.

CareerPilot started as a final-year project, not a company — born from a simple, annoying problem: knowing what to learn next shouldn't take guesswork. Coursework rarely lines up with what job listings actually ask for, and figuring out the gap usually means digging through a dozen tabs at midnight.

So this is that late-night digging, turned into a tool — built by a student who wanted the answer to show up on one screen instead of ten.
