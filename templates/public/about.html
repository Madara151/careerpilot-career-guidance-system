ABOUT CAREERPILOT

A final-year project built to answer one annoying question.

"What should I actually be learning right now?" CareerPilot exists because that question shouldn't take a dozen open tabs and a guess.

Coursework rarely lines up with what job listings actually ask for. CareerPilot exists to close that gap — with real skill data, not vague advice.
— The idea behind CareerPilot
THE PROBLEM

Career planning usually looks like this.

Most students don't lack ambition — they lack a clear, specific answer to "what's missing?" Here's the difference CareerPilot is built to make.

WITHOUT CAREERPILOT

Guessing, one tab at a time

  • Scrolling job listings trying to reverse-engineer what skills matter
  • Picking courses based on what's popular, not what's missing
  • No clear sense of how close you actually are to a target role
  • Resume sits untouched until an application deadline
WITH CAREERPILOT

One dashboard, one clear next step

  • Career requirements are mapped to specific, named skills
  • Every missing skill comes with a matched course to close it
  • A live match percentage shows exactly how close you are
  • Upload a resume once and skills are detected automatically
HOW IT'S BUILT

A few principles this project holds to.

01

Specific over generic

Every recommendation traces back to a named skill and a named career requirement — not a vague "you should learn more."

02

One number you can trust

The match percentage is calculated the same way every time, straight from your skills against real career requirements.

03

Built to keep growing

Careers, skills, and courses are managed and expanded over time — this is a living project, not a one-time build.
